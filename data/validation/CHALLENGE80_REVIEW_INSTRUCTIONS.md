# Blinded challenge review: reviewer instructions

The attached workbook contains 80 model–evaluation relationships not included
in either of the two earlier human-review samples. This is a **classification
reliability challenge**, not a third independent benchmark. It does not change
the existing manuscript results.

Complete the `Blinded review` worksheet without opening the registry class
tables, previous completed review files, manuscript results, or the private
answer key. The workbook deliberately omits initial classes and evidence
grades. Since the registry is public, blinding rests on this review procedure
and the reviewer's dated attestation; it is not technically enforced.

For each row, independently search the model paper/card or checkpoint record,
the benchmark task description, training-data disclosures, and any public
identifier manifest. Record primary-source URLs, a short explanation of the
evidence, and **what was searched even when no exposure was found**. Resolve
the checkpoint/version where possible. Use the frozen
`CLASSIFICATION_GUIDE.md` definitions; the workbook's `Instructions` tab
repeats the essential rules. In particular, common institutional provenance
alone is D1, not D2. A D2 decision needs a cited statement or documented
lineage showing that the training corpus contains the evaluation repository or
cohort. A D3 decision needs the named evaluation dataset in the development
corpus. D4 needs matched case/slide/patch identifiers or equivalent evidence.

Fill every reviewer field for all 80 rows, including the review date, actual
reviewer identity, whether the registry answer was hidden during the initial
decision, and whether AI was used for decisions. Use `D1` for unresolved
evidence, not as proof of nonoverlap. Do not change an initial decision after
discussion; corrections will be added in a separate post-return adjudication
file. If a source is inaccessible or a checkpoint cannot be resolved, say so
explicitly.

Return the completed workbook before seeing the key. No agreement percentage
or manuscript revision should be calculated from a partially completed file.
