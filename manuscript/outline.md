# OncoPretrainMap: An Auditable Registry of Pretraining–Evaluation Dataset Exposure in Cancer Pathology Foundation Models

## Intended article type

Resource Report / Software and Data Resource

## Central claim

Model and benchmark names alone are insufficient to establish independent
evaluation. A versioned lineage registry can separate documented disjointness,
unknown exposure, parent-repository exposure, exact dataset exposure, and exact
case or slide overlap without equating missing disclosure with independence.

## Planned results

1. Scope and completeness of pretraining disclosure across model versions.
2. Dataset-lineage graph and alias resolution.
3. Exposure distribution across a published benchmark matrix.
4. Independent held-out extraction agreement.
5. Exact-manifest positive controls and documented-disjoint negative controls.
6. Checker behavior and reproducibility verification.

## Required restraint

- Do not label repository exposure as memorization.
- Do not call missing evidence disjointness.
- Do not claim exact overlap without identifiers or hashes.
- Do not attribute performance differences causally to exposure.
