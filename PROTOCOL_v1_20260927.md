# OncoPretrainMap Protocol v1

**Frozen:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** Active protocol; changes require a dated deviation record.

## Objective

Construct and validate a versioned registry of evidence that cancer pathology
foundation-model versions were exposed during pretraining, fine-tuning, or
distillation to evaluation datasets, parent repositories, or exact cases,
slides, or derived patches.

## Core universe

The primary core universe contains the 32 model labels evaluated in the 2026
PathBench publication and public repository snapshot at commit
`076ffcef84b7c3359a9ceaeb16e423e567fcd27a`. General vision and vision-language
models are retained because they are explicit comparators in pathology
benchmarks.

Expansion models must satisfy all of the following:

1. intended for pathology or evaluated across at least two cancer-pathology
   tasks;
2. released on or before 2026-09-27;
3. identifiable version or checkpoint;
4. primary paper, technical report, or model card available; and
5. enough information to create a citable model record.

## Unit of analysis

The unit is a specific model/checkpoint version × specific evaluation dataset
version. Model-family names and repository-family names alone are insufficient.

## Source precedence

1. Public training or evaluation manifest with identifiers.
2. Primary paper and supplement.
3. Official model card or developer repository.
4. Benchmark paper or systematic review.
5. Secondary catalog used only for discovery.

Conflicting sources are preserved and flagged; they are not silently resolved.

## Exposure scope

- `D0_documented_disjoint`: explicit, version-relevant exclusion or a temporal
  and identifier manifest demonstrating disjointness.
- `D1_no_detected_evidence_or_insufficient_disclosure`: exposure could not be
  established or excluded.
- `D2_parent_repository_exposure`: a parent collection was used in model
  development and contains the evaluation cohort.
- `D3_exact_dataset_exposure`: the named evaluation dataset was used in model
  development, without exact case-level confirmation.
- `D4_exact_case_slide_or_patch_overlap`: shared identifiers or hashes confirm
  exact content exposure.

## Evidence strength

- `A_identifier_manifest`: direct identifier or hash evidence.
- `B_explicit_primary_source_statement`: explicit statement in a primary source.
- `C_lineage_inference`: reproducible inference from a documented dataset graph.
- `D_incomplete_or_ambiguous_disclosure`: indirect, incomplete, or conflicting
  disclosure.

## Decision rules

1. Exact identifier evidence takes precedence over name-based inference.
2. Parent-repository exposure does not imply exact slide exposure.
3. Missing exposure evidence never establishes independence.
4. A documented-disjoint assertion that conflicts with exposure evidence is
   flagged and not automatically accepted.
5. Pretraining, distillation, fine-tuning, retrieval-corpus, and downstream-
   training exposure are stored separately.
6. “Contamination” is reserved for confirmed exact overlap or a prespecified
   benchmark violation.

## Validation

The extraction schema and rules will be developed on a development subset.
A prespecified held-out subset will be independently and blindly extracted by a
second human reviewer. Original row-level decisions will be preserved. Exact
agreement and unweighted Cohen's kappa will be reported for genuinely blinded
categorical decisions. Unresolved disagreements will remain unresolved or be
adjudicated transparently.

## Primary outputs

- model registry;
- dataset and alias registry;
- dataset-lineage graph;
- model–dataset evidence table;
- derived exposure matrix;
- command-line checker;
- validation report;
- benchmark audit; and
- versioned release with hashes.

## Prohibited claims

The study will not claim that absent evidence proves independence, that parent-
repository exposure proves memorization, or that performance differences are
caused by exposure without an appropriate causal design.
